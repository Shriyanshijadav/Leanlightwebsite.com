A 3 kW rooftop solar system is the most common size for Indian homes, and it is also the size at which the central subsidy reaches its maximum. This guide sets out what it costs, what you pay after subsidy, and how quickly it pays for itself.

## What a 3 kW system costs in 2026

An on-grid 3 kW system costs about **₹1.65 to ₹2.10 lakh** installed, before subsidy. The range covers differences in module and inverter brand, mounting structure and how far the inverter sits from the roof.

| Item | Typical share of cost |
|---|---|
| Solar modules | The largest single item |
| Inverter | The second largest |
| Mounting structure | Depends on roof type and height |
| Cabling, protection and earthing | Fixed for most homes |
| Installation, approvals and net meter | Fixed for most homes |

## What you pay after the subsidy

Under PM Surya Ghar, a 3 kW home system receives **₹78,000**: ₹30,000 for each of the first two kilowatts and ₹18,000 for the third. That brings the net cost to roughly **₹87,000 to ₹1.32 lakh**.

You pay the vendor the full price first. The subsidy is credited to your bank account after the DISCOM inspects the system and installs the net meter. The steps are in our [PM Surya Ghar subsidy guide](/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy/).

## How much it generates and saves

Each kilowatt generates about 4 units a day in Indian conditions, so a 3 kW system produces around **360 units a month**. If you pay ₹8 a unit, that offsets close to ₹2,900 a month, or about ₹34,500 a year.

The saving is highest in the higher tariff slabs, because solar removes your most expensive grid units first.

## Payback

Divide the cost after subsidy by the yearly saving. At ₹8 a unit, a 3 kW system typically pays back in **3 to 5 years**, then keeps generating for the rest of a 25-year module life.

## When 3 kW is the wrong size

- If you use well over 400 units a month, a larger system will save more, although the subsidy stays at ₹78,000. Compare sizes on our [solar system sizes](/solar-system-sizes/) page.
- If power cuts are frequent, consider a [hybrid solar system](/residential-rooftop-solar/hybrid-solar-system/) with a battery.
