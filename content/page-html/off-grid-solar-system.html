Off-grid solar system for sites the grid does not reach

An off-grid solar system is a standalone power system: solar panels charge a battery bank, and the battery runs the building, with no connection to the electricity grid. It is built for farmhouses, remote homes and sites where supply is absent or lasts only a few hours a day.

What is an off-grid solar system?

An off-grid solar system is a solar power plant that operates independently of the utility grid, storing its daytime generation in batteries so that it can supply electricity at any hour.

It has four parts: the solar array, a charge controller or off-grid inverter that manages charging, a battery bank, and the inverter that supplies the building's loads. Because nothing else stands behind it, the system must be designed for the worst week of the year, not the average day. Many installations also include a diesel generator input for long cloudy spells. It connects only to the building it serves.

How an off-grid system works

With no grid to lean on, the battery does the balancing.

The energy flow

During the day the panels supply the building's loads directly and send the rest to the battery. Once the battery is full, the controller holds back the array, so surplus generation is simply not produced. This is why off-grid sizing has to be exact: oversize and you waste capacity, undersize and the lights go out.

At night and in poor weather the battery carries everything. A grid outage means nothing to an off-grid system, because it was never connected. The limit is stored energy, measured in days of autonomy.

Who it suits, and who it does not

A good fit when

  • The site has no grid connection, or extending the line would cost more than the system
  • Supply is available for only a few hours a day
  • You are replacing a diesel generator that runs daily
  • Loads are known and fairly steady: a farmhouse, a pump house, a remote office

Look at another option when

  • You have a working grid connection with occasional cuts: a hybrid solar system gives backup at lower cost and keeps the subsidy
  • Your supply is reliable: an on-grid solar system costs far less and pays back fastest
  • You plan to run heavy loads such as several air conditioners for long hours: the battery cost rises steeply

Off-grid is the right answer to a missing grid, not to a high bill. Where a grid connection exists, one of the other two systems is almost always the better investment.

Cost and payback for an off-grid system

Off-grid is the most expensive of the three system types for the same solar capacity, because it carries a battery large enough to run the building alone and receives no subsidy. The battery is typically the largest single item in the quotation, and with lead-acid chemistry it will need replacing during the life of the panels.

There is no market price per kW that means much, since the cost follows the battery size you need. To confirm: LeanLight Solar indicative price for a 3 kW off-grid system with one day of autonomy. Payback should be measured against what you spend today: diesel, a line extension, or going without power. Against daily generator use, an off-grid system often pays back within a few years. Off-grid solar questions

Can I get a subsidy on an off-grid solar system?

No. The PM Surya Ghar subsidy covers only grid-connected rooftop systems. Standalone battery-based systems are excluded. If you have a grid connection, a hybrid solar system keeps subsidy eligibility.

How many batteries does an off-grid system need?

It depends on your daily consumption and how many days of autonomy you want. A home using 10 units a day needs about 12.5 kWh of usable lithium storage for one day of autonomy.

What happens when it is cloudy for several days?

Generation drops and the battery carries the load until it reaches its cut-off. Systems designed for remote sites include a generator input or extra autonomy for this reason.

Can an off-grid system run air conditioners and pumps?

Yes, if the inverter is rated for the starting current and the battery for the running hours. Motor loads need an inverter with enough surge capacity, which we size from your load list as part of residential rooftop solar design.

How long do off-grid batteries last?

Lithium iron phosphate batteries typically last 10 years or more, tubular lead-acid around 4 to 6 years depending on depth of discharge. Ask to see a working system on our projects page.
