Calculator questions

How accurate is this estimate?

It is a planning estimate. Actual generation depends on your location, roof direction and shade, and actual saving on your tariff slabs. A site assessment replaces each assumption with a measured figure.

Where do I find my rate per unit?

On your bill, divide the energy charges by the units consumed. If your tariff is slabbed, use the rate of the highest slab you reach, since solar removes those units first.

Why does the calculator not cover my whole bill?

Fixed charges remain, and the system is sized to consumption rather than above it, since surplus units earn little in most states.

Does the society estimate show the saving per flat?

Not directly. Divide the yearly saving by the number of flats, then by twelve, for the monthly fall in each flat's maintenance share. The method is explained on the housing society solar page.

Which size is right for me?

The calculator suggests one. Compare it against its neighbours on the solar system sizes page.
