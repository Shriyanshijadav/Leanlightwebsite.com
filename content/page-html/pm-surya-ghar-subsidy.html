PM Surya Ghar subsidy: amounts, eligibility and how to apply

The PM Surya Ghar subsidy is a central government grant of up to ₹78,000 for installing a grid-connected rooftop solar system on a home. It pays ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third, directly to the homeowner's bank account after commissioning.

What is PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana?

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the Government of India's rooftop solar scheme for households, launched in February 2024 and administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy through a national online portal.

Its purpose is to put solar on residential roofs so that a household can generate up to about 300 units of its own electricity each month. The scheme does this with a fixed capital subsidy, called central financial assistance, and with access to low-cost bank loans. The household chooses a registered vendor, the vendor installs the system, the distribution company inspects it and fits a net meter, and the subsidy is then released.

Who is eligible

A household qualifies when all of these are true.

  • The applicant is an Indian citizen
  • The applicant owns a house with a roof suitable for solar panels
  • The house has a valid electricity connection from the local DISCOM
  • The household has not claimed any other solar subsidy
  • The system is grid-connected and installed by a vendor registered on the national portal
  • The modules are from the approved list, made with domestically manufactured cells

What is not covered

  • Commercial and industrial connections
  • Off-grid and standalone battery systems
  • The battery in a hybrid system: only solar capacity counts
  • Systems installed by a vendor who is not registered
  • Capacity above 3 kW, which earns no extra subsidy
  • A second claim by the same household

Documents and loans

Keep these ready: a recent electricity bill, identity proof, proof of ownership of the house, a bank passbook or cancelled cheque, and a photograph of the roof. The name on the electricity connection must match the name of the applicant. A mismatch is the most common reason an application stalls.

The scheme also offers collateral-free loans through public-sector banks for rooftop systems up to 3 kW, applied for through the same portal. Loan amounts and interest rates are set by the bank. To confirm: Current loan terms from partner banks.

PM Surya Ghar questions

How much subsidy will I get for a 3 kW system?

₹78,000: ₹30,000 for each of the first two kilowatts and ₹18,000 for the third. This is the maximum for any household.

When is the subsidy paid?

After the system is installed, inspected and commissioned by the DISCOM, and your bank details are verified. Payment follows in about 30 days.

Is the subsidy deducted from the price I pay the vendor?

No. You pay the vendor the full system price and the subsidy is credited to your bank account afterwards. Budget for the full amount, or use the scheme's loan.

Can a tenant apply?

Only with the connection in the applicant's name and the owner's consent. In practice the scheme is designed for owner-occupiers.

My connection is in my late father's name. Can I apply?

The connection should first be transferred to your name with the DISCOM, since the applicant and consumer names must match.

Can I apply if I already have solar panels?

A capacity addition may qualify for subsidy on the added kilowatts, within the overall 3 kW limit, provided no subsidy was claimed beyond that. How a new system is sized is covered under residential rooftop solar.
