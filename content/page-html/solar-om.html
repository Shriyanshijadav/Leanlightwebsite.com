What solar O&M covers

Solar O&M is everything done to a solar plant after it is switched on to keep it safe and generating at its designed level: cleaning, preventive maintenance, performance monitoring, fault repair and warranty management.

A solar plant has no moving parts, but it does not look after itself. Dust settles on the glass, connectors loosen with heat cycles, a string fuse fails, an inverter fan clogs. Each takes a few percent off output, and without monitoring nobody notices until the bill arrives. O&M exists to find those losses within days.

Why generation falls without it

The commonest cause is soiling. In dry, dusty months a film builds up on the glass within weeks, and bird droppings create hot spots that stress individual cells. A wash restores the output the same day.

The next is undetected faults: one string out of several can be down for months on an unmonitored plant, because the rest keeps generating and the loss looks like weather. Thermal imaging and string-level data catch both.

  • Cleaning is done on a calendar, not when someone remembers
  • Faults raise an alert, with a response time in the contract
  • Warranty claims on modules and inverters are filed by us

Solar O&M questions

How often should solar panels be cleaned in India?

Every two to four weeks in dry months, and less often in the monsoon when rain does part of the job. Sites near construction, highways or farmland need more frequent cleaning.

Can I clean the panels myself?

Yes, for a home system, with plain water and a soft brush in the early morning or evening. Do not use detergent, hard water that leaves scale, or a pressure washer, and never walk on the modules.

Do you maintain systems installed by other companies?

Yes. We start with a health check to establish the plant's condition, then quote for O&M. Contact us through the contact page with the plant size and location.

What is performance ratio?

Performance ratio is actual generation divided by the generation the sunlight on site should have produced. It strips out weather, so a falling ratio means a fault and not a cloudy month.

Is O&M included when I buy a system from you?

To confirm: Period of free service included with a new system. After that, cover continues under an annual contract. New-system scope is described on the residential rooftop solar and housing society solar pages.
