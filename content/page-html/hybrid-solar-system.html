Hybrid solar system: solar, grid and backup

A hybrid solar system is a grid-connected solar system with a battery. It uses solar first, stores the surplus, exports what is left and keeps selected loads running from the battery when the grid fails. It suits homes where power cuts are frequent or long.

What is a hybrid solar system?

A hybrid solar system combines solar panels, a hybrid inverter and a battery, and stays connected to the electricity grid, so a building can draw on three sources: the sun, its own stored energy and the utility.

The hybrid inverter is the part that sets it apart. It converts solar power for the home, charges and discharges the battery, exports to the grid through a net meter and, when the grid fails, isolates the home and forms its own small supply for the circuits wired to the backup output. It connects to the array, the battery, the main distribution board, a separate essential-loads board and the DISCOM meter.

How a hybrid system works

The difference from on-grid shows up at the moment the grid goes down.

The energy flow

On a normal day a hybrid system behaves like an on-grid one with a reserve. Solar runs the home, then charges the battery, then exports. After sunset the battery supplies the home until it reaches its set reserve level, and the grid takes over from there.

When the grid fails, an on-grid system stops. A hybrid inverter disconnects from the grid within a fraction of a second and continues supplying the essential-loads board from solar and battery. When supply returns it reconnects and resumes exporting.

Who it suits, and who it does not

A good fit when

  • Power cuts happen most weeks, or last more than an hour at a time
  • You work from home, run medical equipment or need a refrigerator and fans kept on
  • You want backup and still want subsidy and net metering
  • You would otherwise buy a separate inverter-battery

Look at another option when

  • Outages are rare and short: an on-grid solar system costs far less and pays back faster
  • There is no grid connection at all: an off-grid solar system is designed for that
  • You expect the battery to run the whole house including air conditioners for hours: it can, but the battery cost rises quickly

Decide which circuits are essential before sizing. Backing up lights, fans, a refrigerator, a router and a few sockets needs a far smaller battery than backing up everything.

Cost and payback for a hybrid system

A 3 kW hybrid solar system costs about ₹2.5 to ₹4 lakh in 2026, against ₹1.65 to ₹2.10 lakh for on-grid. The gap is the battery and the hybrid inverter. The central subsidy still applies to the solar capacity, up to ₹78,000, because the system is grid-connected. The battery itself is not subsidised.

A hybrid system saves the same amount on the bill as an on-grid system of equal capacity, so its payback on savings alone is longer. The fair comparison adds what you would otherwise spend on a separate inverter-battery or a generator, and what an outage costs you. Capacities are compared on the solar system sizes page.

Hybrid solar questions

Is a hybrid solar system eligible for subsidy?

Yes, provided it is grid-connected and net-metered. The subsidy is calculated on the solar module capacity only, so the battery does not add to it. Amounts are in the residential rooftop solar cost table.

How long will a hybrid system run my home in a power cut?

That depends on battery size and what is connected. A 5 kWh battery runs about 800 W of essential loads for roughly five hours at night, and longer by day when the panels are producing.

Does a hybrid inverter work without a battery?

Most hybrid inverters can run as grid-tie inverters without a battery and have one added later. Without a battery there is no backup, so the system behaves like an on-grid solar system.

Can I use my existing inverter battery?

Usually not. Hybrid inverters need a compatible battery, most often lithium with a communication link. Existing lead-acid batteries on a home UPS are normally left on their own circuit.

Does the battery charge from the grid?

It can. Most hybrid inverters allow grid charging so the battery is full before an expected outage. Solar charging takes priority by default. See an installed system on the projects page.
