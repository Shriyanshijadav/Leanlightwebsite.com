Solar battery storage for homes

Solar battery storage is a rechargeable battery, usually lithium iron phosphate, added to a rooftop solar system so that surplus daytime energy can be used at night or during a power cut. It can be installed with new solar or retrofitted to a system you already own.

What is solar battery storage?

Solar battery storage is a battery system that stores electricity generated by solar panels for later use, measured in kilowatt-hours of capacity and kilowatts of output.

Capacity, in kWh, tells you how much energy the battery holds. Output, in kW, tells you how many appliances it can run at once. A home battery consists of the cells, a battery management system that protects them, and a hybrid or battery inverter that controls charging and discharging. It connects to the solar inverter or directly to a hybrid inverter, and to a backup distribution board that carries the circuits you want protected.

How battery storage works with solar

A battery shifts solar energy in time: from midday, when you have too much, to the hours when you have none.

The energy flow

Through the day the panels meet the home's demand first. The surplus charges the battery, and only when the battery is full does the system export to the grid. After sunset the battery discharges to run the home until it reaches the reserve level you have set.

In a grid outage the battery inverter isolates the backup circuits from the grid and keeps them supplied. By day the panels continue to recharge the battery during the outage, which is what separates solar storage from an ordinary home UPS.

Who it suits, and who it does not

A good fit when

  • You already have or are installing solar and lose power regularly
  • You need specific loads kept running: medical devices, a home office, refrigeration
  • Your state settles exported units at a low rate, so using your own solar at night is worth more
  • You want to retire a diesel generator or an ageing lead-acid UPS

Look at another option when

  • Outages are rare: an on-grid solar system without storage gives a much faster payback
  • You are starting from scratch and need backup: price a complete hybrid solar system and not a battery on its own
  • You expect storage to pay for itself through bill savings where net metering already credits you one for one

Where net metering gives full credit for exported units, the grid already does the job of a battery at no cost. In those states, buy storage for backup, not for savings.

Cost and payback for battery storage

Market prices in 2026 put a 3 kW hybrid system with a 5 kWh lithium battery at roughly ₹2.8 to ₹3.5 lakh, against ₹1.55 to ₹1.85 lakh for the same solar capacity on-grid. That places the battery and hybrid inverter together at around ₹1.2 to ₹1.7 lakh for 5 kWh. To confirm: LeanLight Solar battery pricing per kWh.

Storage does not increase generation, so it does not shorten payback on the solar system. It earns its place through backup, through replacing generator fuel, and in states where exported units are credited at less than the retail tariff. Where exports earn full credit, the financial return on a battery alone is weak. Size the array first using the solar system sizes page, then add storage for the loads that matter.

Solar battery questions

Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

Yes. An AC-coupled battery inverter can be added alongside most existing grid-tie inverters, or the inverter can be replaced with a hybrid model. We check compatibility from the inverter's model number.

How long does a solar battery last?

Lithium iron phosphate batteries are generally rated for several thousand cycles, which is ten years or more of daily use. The warranty term on the specific battery is stated in your quotation.

Lithium or lead-acid: which should I choose?

Lithium costs more upfront but lasts longer, charges faster, needs no topping up and can be discharged more deeply. Lead-acid remains an option where budget is tight and space is not.

Will a battery let me go completely off the grid?

Not on its own. Running without any grid connection needs a much larger battery and array, designed from the start as a standalone off-grid system.

Is a home solar battery safe?

Lithium iron phosphate is the most thermally stable of the common lithium chemistries, and every battery has a management system that cuts off on over-charge, over-discharge or over-temperature. Installation practice is covered under residential rooftop solar; see examples on the projects page.
