Solar system design and consulting, before you commit to a contractor

Solar system design and consulting is independent engineering for a rooftop or ground-mounted solar project: feasibility, shadow analysis, structural review, electrical design and a generation estimate. LeanLight Solar provides it as a standalone service for owners, architects and developers who want the answers before the tender.

What the service is

Solar design and consulting produces the documents a solar project is built from and judged against: where the panels go, how much they will generate, what the roof must carry and how the plant connects to the building's supply.

It is separate from installation. You can commission the design from us and build with anyone, use it to compare contractors' bids on equal terms, or have us review a design someone else has prepared.

Why shadow comes first

A panel in shade does not only lose its own output. Because panels are wired in series, one shaded module can pull down its whole string. A water tank, a lift room or a neighbouring building can therefore cost far more generation than the area it shades would suggest.

The study on this page shows the method: obstructions cast shadows that move through the day, and the modules they touch are flagged. The layout is then adjusted, or the affected modules are placed on a separate string or fitted with optimisers.

  • Shadows are modelled for summer, winter and the equinoxes
  • Each module gets an annual shade-loss figure
  • The layout that is built is the one that was simulated

Who uses it

Owners of complex roofs

Your terrace has tanks, a lift room, a stair headroom or a taller neighbour. You want to know what the roof can really do and what it should cost before inviting contractors, so that bids are comparable.

Architects and developers

You are designing a new building and want the roof, the electrical room and the cable routes planned for solar from the start, at a fraction of the cost of retrofitting.

Housing societies

Your committee needs an independent report to put to the general body: capacity, cost, subsidy and savings, prepared by someone who is not selling the system.

Can I use your design with another installer?

Yes. The drawings, reports and simulation belong to you once paid for, and you may issue them to any contractor.

How long does a design take?

A feasibility study takes one to two weeks from the site visit. Detailed design for a plant of a few hundred kilowatts takes a further two to three weeks.

What software do you use for generation estimates?

To confirm: Simulation and design software used. Every estimate states its weather data source and each loss assumption, so it can be checked.

Do you review designs prepared by others?

Yes. A design review checks string sizing, cable losses, protection, structural loading and the generation estimate. It is a common step before a committee signs a society contract, described further on the housing society solar page.
