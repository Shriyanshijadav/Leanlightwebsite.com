When does the subsidy actually reach me?

After installation, MSEDCL inspects and commissions the net meter. We then submit your claim on the national portal and it is credited directly to your bank account. We track it until it lands, and you are not chasing anyone.

What happens during the monsoon?

Generation drops on heavily overcast days but does not stop. The figures on this page use a full-year Pune number that already includes the monsoon, so the saving shown is annual, not a best-case month.

How much maintenance does it need?

Mainly cleaning. There are no moving parts in the array. Dust and bird droppings cut output, so we clean on a schedule and check generation each visit.

Can I pay in EMIs?

Yes. Banks offer solar loans for PM Surya Ghar systems, and for many homes the EMI is close to the bill they stop paying. We go through the options at your survey and help with the paperwork.

Is my roof suitable?

You need roughly 100 sq ft of shadow-free space per kW. RCC slabs and metal-sheet roofs both work. The survey checks shade through the day, structural strength and your meter connection before we quote.

What warranties do I get?

Modules carry a manufacturer performance warranty and the inverter carries its own. Your written quote lists each one with its term, and if anything fails we file the claim for you.
